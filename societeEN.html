WHO WE ARE ?

At Once Upon A Time, we believe that every love story is unique and deserves a celebration of its own. Specializing in tailor-made weddings since 2012, we turn your dreams into reality, creating unforgettable events that leave a lasting impression on minds and hearts. Whether you dream of an intimate ceremony on a heavenly beach, a fairytale wedding in a castle, or an original celebration on a theme you're passionate about, we're here to bring your vision to life. Every detail is carefully considered: the choice of venue, decoration, catering, entertainment... Everything is orchestrated with unparalleled precision and creativity.
Our dedicated team makes it a point of honor to understand your desires and exceed your expectations. We work with the best service providers and leverage our expertise to ensure impeccable, stress-free organization, so you can enjoy every moment to the full. At Once Upon A Time, your wedding isn't just an event: it's a true experience, engraved in the memories of you and your loved ones forever. Tell us your story, and let us help you write the most beautiful chapter of your life. Your dream, our mission.

OUR POLICY

At Once Upon A Time, we believe in an eco-responsible and sustainable approach. We are committed to limiting the environmental impact of our activities by favoring local suppliers, recycled materials, and planet-friendly solutions. Every wedding we organize incorporates ethical practices to preserve the beauty of the venues we celebrate. The well-being of our employees is also a top priority. We strive to provide an inclusive and caring work environment, where everyone feels valued and fulfilled. We promote work-life balance by adapting our working hours and encouraging telecommuting whenever possible.
What's more, we make customer satisfaction a point of honor. Our policy is based on transparent communication and active listening to meet our customers' expectations. Each project is tailor-made, with particular attention to detail and originality. Finally, our company is committed to community and charitable actions. A portion of our profits is donated to local associations to support social and environmental causes close to our hearts. At Once Upon A Time, we believe that celebrating love goes hand in hand with a commitment to a better world.
